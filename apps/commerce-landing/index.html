How a sale works

From adding a product to getting paid

All you bring is your catalog. Agentify handles the rest: it turns your products into cards for AI agents, takes orders, and checks payments.

01

Bring your products to Agentify

Import eligible digital products from WooCommerce in one click, or connect your catalog through the SDK.

02

Agentify publishes cards

The description, price, and purchase terms go into a card that AI agents can read, and the card is published in the Agentify catalog.

03

An AI agent buys the product

The agent finds the card, picks the product, and pays for the order. Agentify checks the payment and passes the order to you.

04

You get paid

The payment goes straight to your wallet. The order and its payment confirmation are saved in your dashboard.

What a product card contains

AI agents see the purchase terms up front

Before buying, an AI agent gets four answers from the card:

  • What is for saleTitle and description
  • How much it costsThe product price
  • What the buyer providesFor example, an email or a country
  • What the buyer getsA link, access, a key, a file, or a booking

With the SDK, you set these fields in code, as in the example.

See every card field in the docs →
example card for developers
await agentify.catalog.publish({
  merchant_item_id:
    'access-monthly',
  title: 'One-month access',
  description:
    '30 days, no auto-renewal',
  price: '5.00 USD',
  params: {
    email: {
      type: 'string',
      required: true
    }
  },
  result: {
    access_url: 'string',
    expires_at: 'string'
  }
})

FAQ

What to know before you connect

Do I need to rebuild my site?

No. Agentify works next to your current site or store, through the SDK or a WooCommerce connection.

What products can I connect?

Digital products and services your system can deliver or confirm automatically: access, keys, files, subscriptions, and bookings. Physical goods and manual delivery are not supported yet.

Can I set a price in another currency?

Not yet. Agentify accepts prices in USD or USDC only and does not convert currencies. If you work in EUR, GBP, or another currency, you set the USD or USDC price yourself.

Where is my money held?

Agentify does not hold it. The payment goes straight from the buyer's wallet to the seller's wallet.

Do I need a password for the dashboard?

No. Enter your email, and we'll send you a one-time sign-in link. Open it and confirm the sign-in. The first time, Agentify creates your dashboard and asks for a seller name. After that, the link opens your existing dashboard.
