Twenty short messages to write in a card that goes with a gift, four for
each of five occasions. Copy one as it is or adapt it to the person.


Birthday

1. Happy birthday! I hope this year brings you as much joy as you bring
   everyone around you.
2. Wishing you a relaxed, happy birthday and a year full of good
   surprises.
3. Happy birthday! Here's to more laughter, more adventures and more
   cake.
4. Another year older and still my favorite person to celebrate. Enjoy
   every minute of your day.


Thank you

5. Thank you for your kindness. It meant more than I can say.
6. A small thank-you for a big favor. I'm lucky to know you.
7. Thank you for being there when it mattered. I won't forget it.
8. With thanks for your help and your patience. I really appreciate it.


Wedding

11. Congratulations! May the love you share today grow stronger every

12. Wishing you both every happiness as you start your married life

New baby

14. Congratulations on your new arrival. Wishing you sleep, cuddles and

15. So happy for you all. Your little one is lucky to have you.
16. Congratulations! May your new baby bring you endless smiles.


Sympathy

17. Thinking of you and sending love during this difficult time.
18. With deepest sympathy. I'm here for you whenever you need me.
19. Holding you in my thoughts. May the memories you shared bring you
    comfort.
20. I'm so sorry for your loss. Sending you strength and warm wishes.
