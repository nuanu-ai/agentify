How to wrap a rectangular box neatly and tie a ribbon with a bow.


What you need

- A roll of wrapping paper
- Clear tape
- Sharp scissors
- A ruler or tape measure
- Ribbon: enough to go around the box twice, once each way, plus about
  20 inches (50 cm) for the bow


1. Measure the paper

Unroll the paper face down and place the box upside down on it. Roll the
box over once to find the length you need: enough paper to go all the
way around the box, plus about 2 inches (5 cm) of overlap. For the
width, the paper should reach a little more than halfway up each end of
the box.


2. Cut the paper

Cut along a straight line. If the back of the paper has a printed grid,
follow it. A clean, straight edge is what makes the finished seam look
neat.


3. Wrap the sides

Bring one long edge of the paper up over the box and tape it to the box.
Fold the other long edge under by about half an inch (1 cm) to hide the
cut, pull it snug over the first edge, and tape it along the fold.
Because the box is upside down, the seam ends up on its bottom, where it
will not show.


4. Fold the ends

At one end, push the top flap of paper down against the box and crease
it firmly. Fold the two side flaps in toward the middle, creasing each
one along the edges of the box so that they form triangles. Fold the
remaining bottom flap up over them and tape it in place. Repeat at the
other end.


5. Tie the ribbon

Turn the box right side up. Slide the middle of the ribbon under the box
lengthwise and bring both ends up to the center of the top. Cross them
there, give them a quarter turn, and take them down the other two sides.
Cross them again under the box, bring them back up to the center, and
tie a firm knot.


6. Tie the bow

Make a loop with each end of the ribbon, cross the loops, and tie them
together the way you tie a shoelace. Pull the loops to even them out,
then cut each tail at an angle so that it does not fray.
